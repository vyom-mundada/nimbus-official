01 — Introduction

The water crisis is not a resource problem. Earth has more than enough water.

For a century, water infrastructure depended on geography — rivers, aquifers, coastlines.

2.1 billion people still lack access to clean water, not because it doesn't exist, but because we never built systems to extract it from air.

That changes now.

02 — The Science

Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs) are engineered materials with ultra-high surface area capable of capturing water molecules from air.

They can extract water even at very low humidity using solar heat regeneration.

Specs

MOF-303: 0.7–1.0 L/kg/day

Activation: 10% humidity

Temp: 60–80°C

03 — What We’re Building

Modular atmospheric water units that generate up to 5,000 litres per day using air and sunlight.

AI selects optimal MOF configuration based on local climate data.
