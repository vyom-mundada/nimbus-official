P1 — Introduction

We want to be honest with you about something most climate companies won't say out loud.

The water crisis is not a resource problem. Earth has more than enough water. The atmosphere alone holds 12.9 quadrillion litres of it, distributed evenly, perpetually replenished, present above every city, village, and desert on the planet. The water exists. The problem is that we've never been able to reach it.

For a century, our entire model of water infrastructure has been built around geography. You need to be near a river. Near a coastline. Near an aquifer that hasn't been drained yet. If you're not, you wait. You walk kilometres. Your children get sick, and miss school.

2.1 billion people are still waiting. Not because the water isn't there, but because nobody has built the machine to take it from something everyone has available: the air.

That machine exists now. We're building it.

P2 — The Science that Changes Everything

In the late 1990s, a chemist named Omar Yaghi at UC Berkeley began engineering a new class of materials he called Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs).

The idea was deceptively simple: build crystalline, nanoporous structures whose geometry you could tune at the atomic level, creating materials with surface areas larger than a football field, packed into a single gram of powder.

For two decades, MOFs lived in academia. Then researchers asked a different question: what if you designed a MOF whose pores were sized exactly right to trap water molecules from air, even desert air at 10% humidity, and then release them as liquid with only gentle heat?

Technical Specifications
Material: MOF-303
0.7 - 1.0 L/kg/day
Activation Threshold
10% Relative Humidity
Regeneration Temp
60–80°C Solar Thermal

In October 2025, Omar Yaghi was awarded the Nobel Prize in Chemistry. The science is done. What remains is engineering and will.

P3 — What We’re Building

"Hardware is commodity. Intelligence is moat."

Nimbus builds modular atmospheric water harvesting units that ship in containers, deploy in 24 hours, and produce up to 5,000 litres of clean water per day using air and sunlight.

But the device is not the company. The intelligence layer is. We built an AI-driven MOF selection engine that:

  • • Ingests GPS + climate data
  • • Maps humidity, temperature, irradiance
  • • Matches against MOF performance curves
  • • Outputs optimal configuration per location

Post-deployment IoT data continuously improves system performance. Hardware is commodity. Intelligence is moat.
